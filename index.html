A Few Frames

Five little pieces from the days we shared.

01 / 05

THE FIRST SHOT

The beginning, quite naturally.

I think what first caught my attention was your intro. It felt like we might actually match, and somehow, talking to you just flowed.

From films to random campus complaints, we somehow managed to talk about everything without it ever feeling forced.

02 / 05

SOMEWHERE BETWEEN

Somewhere between teasing and blushing.

You had this habit of calling me cute, and somehow, I was always a little too easy to make flustered.

Then came the little “kakak” calls, which somehow made the conversations even sweeter.

And yes, you called me pretty too. I definitely remembered that one.

03 / 05

LITTLE THINGS

You made the quiet moments feel less quiet.

You are the kind of person who pays attention to little things.

And whenever I got excited about something, you somehow got excited with me. Somehow, I never felt like I was talking alone.

04 / 05

MY FAVORITE SHOT

Stories traded back and forth.

Maybe this one is my favorite.

Trading stories, trading complaints, trading film titles — little pieces of our days passing between two people who had only just met.

Somehow, those simple exchanges became some of the moments I wanted to keep.

05 / 05

A LITTLE NOTE

Before the last frame.

Thank you for being my chat companion for these past few days.

Thank you for listening to all my little complaints, random stories, and everything in between. I am genuinely glad I got to know you.

I hope somehow, somewhere, our paths cross again. Until then, take care of yourself, okay? Keep going, stay healthy, and please — eat something good every day.

Until another frame finds us.
